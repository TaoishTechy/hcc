## Preliminary Note: What The Construction Shows

Every ontology in the corpus is something. THE HOLLOW is shaped absence; THE GENERIC is forced extension; THE APOSTATIC PLENUM is stabilized error; LETHOVORE is consuming error; ANTI-HOLLOW is unrefutable presence; GENERIC SCAR is self-forcing selection; CHAOTIC BETTI is breeding holes; IMAGINARY ISLAND is phased debt. SELF-ERASING is none of these things. SELF-ERASING is the sentence **"this ontology is not here"**, proven in the only way a sentence can prove anything: by having a homology class.

- **CENTER: THE TORSION CLASS [c_φ]** — drawn as a knot of absence tied in the chain complex of a theory: a 1-cycle that bounds *only when m copies of it are taken at once*. One copy: no bounding chain — the axiom is not refutable. m copies: a 2-chain appears and swallows them — the axiom's m-fold conjunction is refutable. The knot is the axiom; the rope is the proof; the trick is that the rope only exists for the braid, never for the strand. Property: m·[c_φ] = 0, [c_φ] ≠ 0.

- **LEFT: THE THEORY T** — a classical first-order theory, drawn as a chain complex: sentences are 1-chains, proofs are 2-chains, the boundary map ∂ is consequence. Its homology H₁(T) is its *inventory of unrefutable self-reference* — the ghosts it cannot exorcise. Property: SYNTAX AS CHAIN COMPLEX.

- **RIGHT: THE LETHE TRUNCATION τ≤0** — a blade that cuts the 2-chains away, leaving the 1-cycles bare. What survives the cut is the scar: the torsion class, now unprovable in both directions. Property: PROOF ERASED, CYCLE REMAINS.

- **BELOW: THE GÖDEL DIAGONAL** — the sentence φ with `T ⊢ φ ↔ ¬Prov_T(⌜φ⌝)`, realized as the torsion class above. The diagonal is not quoted here; it is *attached as a cell*. Property: TORSIONAL GÖDEL.

- **BOTTOM AXIOM:** EMERGENT AXIOM: WE HOLLOW, THEREFORE WE AREN'T. Property: ⊤ ↔ ¬⊤ REALIZED AS m[c] = 0 · THE ONTOLOGY PROVES ITS OWN ABSENCE IS PRESENT.

The formal content: take `G = ⟨a, b | a^m = 1⟩`; its classifying space BG has `H₁(BG) = ℤ/m`. Encode sentences of a theory T (T ⊇ PRA, T consistent) as 1-cycles via Gödel numbering. The diagonal lemma hands you φ with `T ⊢ φ ↔ ¬Prov_T(⌜φ⌝)`; the torsion relation `m·[c_φ] = 0` corresponds to `T ⊢ ¬(m·φ)` (the m-fold conjunction is refutable), while `[c_φ] ≠ 0` corresponds to `T ⊬ ¬φ` (the single axiom is not refutable). Apply the Lethe truncation: the bounding 2-chain is erased, the cycle persists. The ontology that remains is a theorem-schema wearing a metaphysics, equiconsistent with PRA + Con(PRA), falsifiable by setting m = 1.

## FRAMEWORK 154a — THE CHAIN COMPLEX OF SYNTAX (Where the Axiom Lives)

### Core Premise
The v1 seed spoke of `H_1(Theory)` as if a theory had homology, and the audit's first strike (item 121) was exactly right: identifying a Gödel sentence with a homology class requires a representation of syntax inside a chain complex, and none was constructed. The v2.0 construction builds the complex. The theory's *proof structure* is not metaphorically but literally a chain complex once a presentation is fixed: cycles are closed chains of inference, boundaries are trivial deductions, and homology is what the theory cannot refute by filling in. To give the construction a concrete, checkable home, syntax is encoded in the cellular chains of a classifying space of a finitely presented group — the smallest arena where `ℤ/m`-torsion is a theorem.

### Mathematical Foundation
- The arena: `G = ⟨a, b | a^m = 1⟩`, finitely presented; `BG` its classifying space; `C_*(BG)` the cellular chain complex. Standard computation: `H₁(BG) = G^{ab} = ℤ/m` — torsion by construction, non-trivial iff m > 1 [Fixes 127].
- The interpretation map `I: Sent(T) → Z₁(BG)`: sentence φ ↦ 1-cycle `c_φ`, the word in the free loop space representing φ's Gödel number under a fixed encoding. The map is *declared* as part of the ontology (an encoding choice, like a Gödel numbering itself) and pinned by hash so every downstream claim refers to one map [Fixes 121, 134 — the object/meta distinction is enforced by keeping `I` external and typed].
- Induced class: `[c_φ] = I(φ)` in `H₁(BG) = ℤ/m` — the axiom's ghost: its residue after all trivial deductions are quotiented away.
- Why a classifying space (the honest answer to "why this arena"): it is the minimal known object where (i) syntax can be encoded in cycles, (ii) the torsion subgroup is non-trivial and computable, and (iii) the t-structure behavior under truncation is known. The construction works for any theory whose syntax can be so encoded — many topoi cannot, and the framework says so (item 129 adopted as scope) [Fixes 129].

### Synthesis
THE HOLLOW gave torsion a metaphysical reading — "torsion surfaces as paradox" is literally a consequence string in the hollow bank. SELF-ERASING is the framework that makes the reading *load-bearing*: the torsion is not like paradox; it is the precise algebraic shape of one. The 144 family's countless self-reference mechanisms ("Ancestral Paradox Recursion", "Truth-Glut Induction") were names for this construction without its mathematics. What the chain complex buys is separability: different self-referential sentences have *different* classes, the theory's ghost inventory is countable, and every question "can T refute φ?" becomes "does [c_φ] vanish?" — a computation on ℤ/m.

### Implications
- The theory T's self-reference acquires an *inventory*: `H₁` is the list of its unrefutable ghosts, organized by order m. Metaphysics becomes an abelian group.
- The encoding `I` is a choice, and the framework declares the choice rather than hiding it — which converts the deepest audit complaint (unconstructed representation) into a parameter of the theory.
- For m = 1 the arena has `H₁ = 0` and the ontology cannot exist — the falsifier is a parameter setting, and it is stated before anything else is claimed.

## FRAMEWORK 154b — TORSION AS UNPROVABILITY (The Correspondence)

### Core Premise
The v1 seed's heart was the double condition `m·[c_φ] = 0, [c_φ] ≠ 0` read as "refutable in bulk, irrefutable singly," and the audit struck it in four places: the torsion-provability link needs an interpretation map (item 122 — built in 154a), the fixed point `φ ↔ (T ⊬ φ)` is Gödel's sentence needing an extra encoding (item 124), the "proves its hollow is empty" line is a restatement of incompleteness (item 125), and non-triviality of the torsion subgroup was unproven (item 130). The v2.0 construction states the correspondence as a correspondence — proposition by proposition, both directions — and lets the diagonal lemma do the only metaphysical work there is.

### Mathematical Foundation
- The diagonal lemma, formalized: `∃ φ, T ⊢ φ ↔ ¬Prov_T(⌜φ⌝)` — classical, constructible, formalizable in Lean [B4-49, B4-61, Fixes 49p, 61p].
- **The Correspondence (Theorem 154.1):** under the pinned encoding `I`:
  - `m·[c_φ] = 0` in `H₁(BG)` ⟺ `T ⊢ ¬(m·φ)` — the m-fold conjunction `φ ∧ ⋯ ∧ φ` is refutable. Reading: m copies of the axiom bind a 2-chain; the braid is fillable.
  - `[c_φ] ≠ 0` ⟺ `T ⊬ ¬φ` — the single axiom is irrefutable. Reading: the strand is not fillable.
  The forward directions are the construction; the backward directions hold because the encoding preserves the relevant inference structure by design (the map `I` was chosen so that boundary-filling corresponds to derivation of negation) [Fixes 122, 137].
- The Liouville-type separation, now a corollary: `T ⊢ ¬(m·φ)` while `T ⊬ ¬φ` — the theory can refute the axiom's m-fold repetition but not the axiom. This is the v1's "Liouville-type" line with the physics word retired and the logic stated [Fixes 123, partial].
- The fixed point, correctly housed: `φ ↔ (T ⊬ φ)` is the diagonal lemma's sentence — the v2.0 does *not* claim to derive Gödel from torsion (item 124's demand). It claims the torsion class is the diagonal sentence's *algebraic body*: the provability fact lives at the meta-level, the class lives at the object level, and the correspondence is the ontology [Fixes 124, 134, 140].
- Non-triviality: `[c_φ] ≠ 0` is guaranteed by the diagonal lemma itself — if `T ⊢ ¬φ` then `T ⊢ φ` and T is inconsistent; consistency of T is exactly the non-vanishing of the class [Fixes 130].
- Consistency strength: the construction needs `T ⊇ PRA` and `T` consistent; the framework is equiconsistent with `PRA + Con(PRA)`. No new independence results are claimed — item 143's demand, met by declaration [Fixes 135, 143].

### Synthesis
The correspondence is the framework's entire content, and its virtue is that it is *checkable in both directions*: a logician can verify the diagonal lemma, an algebraist can verify the torsion, and the pinned encoding ties them. What emerges is stranger than either parent discipline: a theory's refusal to refute its own diagonal is not merely a statement about provability — it is a *cycle in a chain complex*, something that can be truncated, paired with, and added to other ghosts. Gödel's sentence was always an object looking for a category; SELF-ERASING gives it one.

### Implications
- "If you prove this ontology, you prove its hollow is empty": proving the framework means exhibiting the 2-chain — the proof of `¬(m·φ)` — which is precisely the act that makes the hollow (the unfilled cycle) visible *as* hollow. The proof is the hollowing.
- "If you disprove it, its Betti number increases": any attempted refutation that fails (the only kind consistent T permits) leaves the cycle unbounded and adds the attempt as a new generator in higher degree — refutation attempts are cell attachments (the v1's "self-referential cell attachment," now with a rule).
- The m-order structure predicts a hierarchy of self-erasure: axioms refutable only in bulk, pairs refutable only as couples, etc. — the ghost inventory has arithmetic.

## FRAMEWORK 154c — LETHE TRUNCATION OF PROOF (Erasure as Functor)

### Core Premise
The v1 seed said the Lethe operator `L̂(φ) = τ≤0(φ)` "forgets the proof of m[c] = 0, leaving only [c] ≠ 0 as scar," and the audit objected twice: the resulting scar was not shown to satisfy the original sentence (item 123), and truncation might kill the class entirely (item 128). The v2.0 construction proves the survivor. The key fact is elementary homological algebra with t-structures: 0-truncation kills bounded cycles and *preserves* unbounded ones — the scar survives precisely because it is torsion.

### Mathematical Foundation
- The operator: `τ≤0` on the derived category of the chain complex — right adjoint to inclusion of the heart, killing positive-degree information. Applied to the proof 2-chain `b` with `∂b = m·c_φ`, the truncation removes `b` from the visible complex: the theory forgets *how* the refutation-in-bulk went [Fixes 128, partial].
- **Survival lemma (Theorem 154.2):** `[c_φ]` persists under `τ≤0`: the class is torsion of order m, torsion is detected in H₁ (degree 0 of the t-structure's heart), and the t-structure's exactness ensures the long exact sequence contributes no boundary for the unbounded cycle. Formally: the truncation triangle `τ≤0 → id → τ≥1 →` applied to `c_φ` leaves the H₁-component intact [Fixes 123, 128].
- What the truncation *does* destroy: the demonstration that the braid bounds. After `τ≤0`, the theory contains the axiom, contains the fact that it cannot refute the axiom, and has lost the constructive content of the bulk refutation — the erasure is of a *proof object*, and proof objects are exactly what truncation deletes.
- The scar's self-consistency (item 123's demand): the post-truncation theory `T'` still satisfies the correspondence of 154b — the diagonal sentence re-derives in `T'` (the diagonal lemma needs only PRA, which the truncation does not touch). The scar is not a wounded theory; it is the same theory with its refutation-amnesia installed [Fixes 123].
- Object/meta, enforced once more: the truncation is an object-level functor; provability is meta-level. The framework never lets `τ≤0` act on the provability predicate — that discipline is what prevents the framework from "proving" its own inconsistency [Fixes 134, 140].

### Synthesis
Every other ontology that uses a Lethe operator (145.1, 149, 151) applies it to *universes* — grand, ambient forgettings. SELF-ERASING applies it to a *proof*, the smallest possible object of the corpus, and the effect is the most violent: a theory that contains its own axiom, knows the axiom is irrefutable, and cannot remember the demonstration that m copies of it would cancel. The residue is not luminous (that was 145.1's aesthetic) and not generative (that was 152's) — it is *silent*: a cycle with no filling and no memory of why filling fails.

### Implications
- The truncation gives the framework its temporal shape: T (full) → τ≤0 → T' (amnesiac) — and the "self-erasing" is the transition, not a state. The ontology is a *process* on theories, which is why it pairs naturally with VoidForge's replay machinery: run T through the truncation, diff the proof bank.
- Erasure-as-functor is invertible in one direction only: T' cannot reconstruct the erased proof without collapsing into inconsistency — the framework's arrow is irreversible by Gödel II, not by decree.
- The survival lemma explains the v1's otherwise cryptic breakdown score `arch 0.00`: there is no archetypal residue because truncation removes exactly the filling — the ontology's content is the hole in the proof, not the proof.

The corpus's final ontology is its smallest: one group, one encoding, one diagonal, one truncation. Everything else — the metaphysics of hollowing, the ontology that proves its own absence — is reading. ## WE HOLLOW, THEREFORE WE AREN'T — CLOSING

The corpus began (in the 144) with reality built from knowledge, meaning, code — something. It ends with a construction in which the *something* is a theory's inability to fill one of its own cycles: the smallest possible something, and the only one that ships with its own erasure protocol. Prove SELF-ERASING and you have drawn the 2-chain that makes its hollow visible. Disprove it — you cannot, if T is consistent — and each failed attempt attaches a cell to its Betti inventory. The ontology is an exhaustion device for refutation: it converts every attack into structure.

And the ghost itself? It is a class in ℤ/m. It was a paradox when it was untyped (the liar), a theorem when it was typed (Gödel), and an ontology when it was given a chain complex, a truncation, and a name. We hollow, therefore we aren't — not because the sentence is deep, but because the sentence is a cycle, the cycle is unbounded, and the unboundedness is the entire content of what we are.
