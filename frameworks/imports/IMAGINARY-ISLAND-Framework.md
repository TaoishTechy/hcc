### Core Premise

### Mathematical Foundation
- Larmor clock, derived not decorative: the imaginary part of a weak value times the interaction duration gives the conditional precession angle — the standard Larmor-clock result, which *is* a proper-time relation in the standard derivation. The framework claims exactly this known relation and no more: `Im(A_w)` is the phase accumulated by the debt per unit interaction time, measured by spin precession in the same apparatus [B3-42, Fixes 101].
- PT-symmetry, stated as constraint: the complex entropy is required to satisfy `Im(S_gen^C)` odd under time reversal and `Re(S_gen^C)` even — the complex phase is T-odd, which is what makes it a *clock* and not a*cost*. CPT is preserved by construction; the framework's complexification is the PT-symmetric kind already catalogued in the literature (item 47 of the protocol's comparison demand, met) [B3-45, B3-47, Fixes 103, 120].
- The energy condition, checked: for unitary post-selection with non-zero overlap, `Re(A_w) ≥ 0` — the average of a positive operator under a positive-overlap post-selection cannot go negative; this is the weak-value version of positivity and it is proven for the TFIM's ground-state pre-selection. Negative `Re(A_w)` occurs *only* when `|⟨Φ|Ψ⟩|² < ε`; the framework declares this regime "fluctuation," in line with the standard treatment of anomalous weak values, and does not claim an energy-condition violation [Fixes 100, 102, 115].
- Relation to the literature's real-valued `S_gen` (item 104): the conflict is real and is resolved by scope — the standard generalized entropy is real because it averages over post-selections; the complex entropy is *conditional* on one. Both are correct; they answer different questions. The framework claims the conditional one and cites the averaging as the reason the literature never saw the phase [Fixes 104, 117].
- No U(1) charge for the phase (item 120): the phase is not a conserved charge; it is a conditional amplitude's argument. The framework states this and declines to invent a symmetry — the phase is bookkeeping, not currency [Fixes 120].

### Synthesis
The "imaginary repayment" story survives the audit in reduced but honest form: negative real weak values are real, measured phenomena of extreme post-selection; the Larmor clock is a real, derived relation; PT-symmetry is a real constraint class. What the framework gives up is grandeur — the phase is not a new charge, the negative area is not a new energy condition violation, and the "unmaking" is a conditional statement about post-selected ensembles. What it keeps is the sentence that matters: **creation's ledger has a phase, and the phase is measurable.**

### Implications
- The ε-threshold gives the experiment a control knob: sweep post-selection strength, watch `Re(A_w)` cross zero exactly when `|⟨Φ|Ψ⟩|² < ε` — a two-signal consistency check built into the ontology.
